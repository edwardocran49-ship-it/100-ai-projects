# My Working Manifesto for Responsible AI

**Edward Ocran**

## My philosophy

I will use intelligent systems to expand human judgment, not to conceal or replace it. Useful work begins with a clearly stated problem, evidence that can be checked, and respect for the people affected by the result.

## What these systems should be

They should be understandable enough to challenge, proportionate to the decision, secure with the information entrusted to them, and accessible to the people expected to use them. Performance without accountability is incomplete.

## What I will always do

1. I will identify the decision, the evidence, and the person accountable for the outcome.
2. I will test results against meaningful baselines and disclose uncertainty.
3. I will protect private information and collect only what the work genuinely needs.
4. I will document data provenance, limitations, evaluation choices, and material changes.
5. I will preserve human review and a route to question consequential results.
6. I will test for uneven performance across relevant groups and operating conditions.

## What I will never do

I will not present guesses as facts, hide a known limitation, manufacture evidence, misuse confidential data, or automate a high-impact decision simply because automation is available. I will not accept convenience as a substitute for consent, safety, or due process.

## The future I want to help build

I want capable tools to make expertise more available while keeping responsibility visible. The best systems should help people investigate, create, and decide with greater clarity. Their success should be measured not only by speed or accuracy, but also by whether people can understand, contest, and safely benefit from their use.

## My commitments for the next year

7. I will maintain a documented evaluation suite for every substantial model I publish.
8. I will review this manifesto against real project decisions at least once each quarter.
9. I will record one concrete improvement after each review and carry it into the next project.
