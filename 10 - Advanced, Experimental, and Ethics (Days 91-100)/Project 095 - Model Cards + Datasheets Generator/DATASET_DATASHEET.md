# Datasheet: Triage Benchmark

## Motivation and composition

Support reproducible evaluation of triage ranking. The release contains 12,500 rows and 18 features.

## Collection and preprocessing

Records were collected under documented consent and access controls. Identifiers were removed and transformations were versioned.

## Recommended uses and exclusions

Use for evaluation and controlled research; do not re-identify individuals.

## Maintenance

The owner reviews provenance, access, and retention quarterly.
